Leaving the UAE permanently: common questions

What should I do first when leaving the UAE permanently?

Most exit guides put employer notice and your final settlement / end-of-service gratuity first. UAE labour law expects end-of-service dues within a short window after the contract ends (commonly cited as 14 days under Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021; confirm the current text). Then cancel dependent visas, then the sponsor's residence.

How long is the grace period after UAE residence visa cancellation?

It depends on your category. ICP lists grace periods of 30, 60, 90 or 180 days, and GDRFA Dubai states 60 days. The figure printed on your own cancellation document is the one that counts, so check it before you book travel.

Do dependent visas have to be cancelled before the sponsor's visa?

Yes. Dependent residence visas must be cancelled before the sponsor's own residence is cancelled. For employees, residence cancellation is usually employer-led through MOHRE and then GDRFA or ICP; self-sponsored residents start it themselves.

What should I clear before leaving the UAE for good?

The common blockers are unpaid traffic, Salik or vehicle fines, an open DEWA account, an uncleared Ejari tenancy registration, telecom equipment not returned, and closing your last bank account before your final settlement and deposit refunds have landed.

What happens if I overstay after my UAE visa is cancelled?

Overstay fines apply once the grace period ends. A figure of around AED 50 per day is commonly reported, but confirm the current amount on official channels (ICP or GDRFA) and on your cancellation paper.
