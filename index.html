Why this exists

  • Exit guides consistently put employer notice + final settlement / gratuity first; UAE labour law expects end-of-service dues within a short window after the contract ends (commonly cited as 14 days under Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 — confirm current text).
  • Dependent visas must be cancelled before the sponsor’s residence; residence cancel is usually employer-led via MOHRE then GDRFA/ICP (self-sponsored people initiate themselves).
  • Practical blockers that strand people: unpaid traffic / Salik / vehicle fines, open DEWA accounts, uncleared Ejari, telecom equipment not returned, and closing the last bank before settlements and refunds land.
  • After residence cancel you typically get a grace period (often ~30 days — confirm on your document) before overstay fines apply (commonly reported around AED 50/day — confirm officially).
